Kind Reminders

There is no perfect score. The test is computer-adaptive, meaning questions get harder or easier based on answers, so there is no maximum or "100%" score.

Growth matters most. Typical academic growth per term ranges from 2 to 8 points, and progress over time is more important than a single score.

RIT score is a proprietary adaptive scoring index of NWEA MAP. It has been used here as an approximate only.
